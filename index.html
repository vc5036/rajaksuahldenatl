ABOUT THE CENTRE

Dental Care Built Around Your Comfort

Raj Kaushal Dental & Orthodontics Centre is equipped with advanced equipment and follows modern dental treatment techniques. Every treatment plan begins with a careful understanding of the patient's concern.

Patient comfort and convenience are a priority. Sterilization is carried out using a modern B Class Autoclave Sterilizer, and the campus offers a spacious common waiting hall, a special VIP room, in-house parking and a solar power plant for uninterrupted eco-friendly care.
